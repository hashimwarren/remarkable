# Evidence Quality and Visible Proof

Set the proof burden before gathering support. For every major claim ask:

> What would persuade an informed skeptic, and what would weaken this claim?

## Grade the evidence

Prefer direct records, primary data, product behavior, or original documents for facts; first-party research with inspectable methods for findings; independent sources for external validation; and transparent examples or reasoning when empirical proof is not the claim type.

Grade sources by proximity to the fact, methodological transparency, independence, recency, and fit to the exact population and claim. Several sources repeating one origin remain one piece of evidence. Search adversarially for counterevidence, competing explanations, boundary cases, and conditions under which the claim fails.

## Validate data

Before using a number, check its definition, unit, grain, denominator, time period, sample, missing data, baseline, calculation, and selection effects. Keep correlation distinct from causation. If the data cannot support the desired wording, change the wording.

## Make proof visible

For every major supported claim ask:

> What would let the reader inspect this for themselves?

Use the strongest fitting rung:

1. **Show it:** video, screenshot, demonstration, or before-and-after.
2. **Measure it:** chart, benchmark, analytics, or a data table.
3. **Let someone else say it:** customer quotation, review, or expert testimony.
4. **Explain it:** mechanism, concrete example, or logical demonstration.

Recommend only assets with an evidentiary job. Do not add decorative media as proof. Read [informational-images.md](informational-images.md) to distinguish Legitimacy and Numbers proof from Explanation and Steps. In this release, create specifications rather than media unless the user explicitly asks for production: screenshot shot lists, recording scripts, chart specifications, data tables, diagram plans, captions, annotations, and alt text.

Place an asset placeholder next to the claim it supports, state what the asset must demonstrate, and remove unused placeholders before final handoff.
